          Microphone Permission Required

          To record voice notes inside the Burgeroov App, you need to manually allow microphone access in your phone settings.

          How to enable:
          1. Open your phone's Settings app.
          2. Go to Apps (or App Management).
          3. Select the Burgeroov App.
          4. Tap Permissions and turn on the Microphone switch.
          5. Reopen the Burgeroov App!
